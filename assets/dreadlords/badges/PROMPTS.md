# Badge prompt set

## Shared direction

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Roblox achievement badge icon, square master cropped to a circle. Premium hand-painted 3D game UI icon with chunky Roblox-like forms, a dark Saturday-morning fantasy tone, a crisp readable silhouette, and polished mobile-game achievement rendering. Center the emblem, keep all important details inside the inner 72% circular safe area, leave a dark margin, and make it readable at 150 px. Use a circular dark-iron medallion border with four small bone-like studs, a subtle violet outer glow, and a deep radial vignette. Use charcoal, midnight navy, bone ivory, royal purple, ectoplasm green, and antique gold. No words, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, gore, wounds, realistic horror, rotting flesh, or red blood.
