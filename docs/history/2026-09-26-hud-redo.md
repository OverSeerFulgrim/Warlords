# The HUD redo: nothing on screen until he needs it (2026-09-26, late)

After blueprints made run one start with nothing to build, the old HUD — a full-width bottom bar
of Research / Bounty / Workforce / build buttons, a stockpile strip, a log pane — showed a dozen
things the player could not use yet. Designer's rulings, asked and answered:

1. **Mockups first** — the "Warlords HUD redo" design canvas (seven artboards: Main, Field, Home,
   Items, Unlock, Ladder, Map). Built from those.
2. **The rule of the HUD: every piece appears the first time its mechanic does.** The Ladder
   artboard is the chart; `verify_hud` walks it.
3. "Is there anything to show your undead?" → **a roster of the dead with health**, click to select.
4. "Under the Day in upper right … a History button."
5. "A button to get a large map … points of interest labeled when discovered … probably the M key."
   M opens the full map; the corner minimap stays (no key hides it any more — "thats fine").

## What changed

- **No bottom bar.** Everything floats over the world; the camera's bottom inset is 0.
  `Main._build_hud()` replaced `_build_bottom_shell()`.
- **`scripts/ui/HudStyle.gd`** (new): the palette (BG #17131d, BORDER #3b3247, the accent) and
  `box/panel/style_button/style_chip/label/bar/set_bar/hp_color`. New HUD pieces use it; nothing
  else should hand-roll a StyleBox.
- **`HudTopBar`** rewritten as two columns.
  - Left: the **portrait card** (name, `Lv N`, hp bar, stance chip, bag chip `items n/6`, escort
    chip); the **worn-gear strip** (five slot buttons, green when worn — appears once he owns
    gear); the **"Not home yet"** line (resources in his pack — appears when he carries some);
    the orientation line on a dark panel; the dusk warning.
  - Right: Day and phase, threat (only above 0), **Map (M)** and **History (L)** under the clock,
    the guild standing chip (after he first reaches the guild's door), the Raven chip (after the
    first ping). The speed button is debug-only as before.
  - The **Treasury** is top-centre and only near the Throne (`home_provider`, 6 cells).
- **`DeadRoster.gd`** (new): left column, under the card. Escort first, then the rest; name,
  health bar, hp; wounded rows red; `+ N more` past six. Click → select that unit (panel + camera).
  Hidden until the first dead rise. Polled at 0.25 s.
- **`ActionBar.gd`** (new): bottom-centre. Raise Dead (R) and Items (I) from the first minute;
  **Escort (E)** after the first dead; **Rally point (C)** at three dead; **Build (B)** once any
  blueprint is known. Build opens a tray above the bar (`BuildMenu.build_row_into`, styled
  "Name / cost" buttons, Demolish).
- **`LogTicker.gd`** (new): the last four log lines, fading after 9 s, above the action bar. The
  full log is the **History window** (L, `HudWindow`), which also holds the old command-history.
- **`HudWindow.gd`** (new): a centred, titled, closable window; History and Workforce use it.
  Workforce (the old EconomyTab) and **Flee the region** are buttons that appear only by the Throne.
- **`MapScreen.gd`** (new, M): a full-window `Minimap` on its own CanvasLayer with a names layer
  and a list of **known places** — his lair and the guild (with standing) from the start, the
  village once any of it has been seen, every discovered site (houses excluded) tagged
  `guarded / plans / N items left / emptied`. **Only what he has seen is drawn or named**
  (`WorldSite.discovered`, the Raven's flag). Left-click or a list entry: look there and close;
  right-click: walk him there. Checkboxes for names, the Raven's marks, his dead. Does not pause.
- **Blueprint banner:** learning a blueprint shows a centred "New blueprint" banner.
- **Inspector** now opens on the right under the right column; the old `_select_info` bar is gone.
- **`WorkerToken`**: an overhead hp bar when a unit is wounded or rallied.
- **`Controls`**: `escort` E, `rally` C, `build` B, `map` M, `history` L; the `minimap` toggle
  action is gone.
- Fixes on the way: "A An outlaw" (`Main._a_name()` article helper); the opening popup raised
  clear of the ticker; the minimap legend reads "… · M: full map".

## Harnesses

## Not done / next

- The HUD is sized for 1400×800 and up; nothing yet re-lays it for small windows.
- The roster selects; it does not yet order (no unit orders — by design) or show what a unit is doing
  beyond "walking with him / at work" in the tooltip.
- Human playtest of the new HUD with a real mouse is the last word (headless and Xvfb screenshots
  only so far).
