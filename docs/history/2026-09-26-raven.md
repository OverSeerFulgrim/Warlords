# The bird that never lies (R2e, 2026-09-26)

`RAVEN_SPEC.md` built as amended, plus the two R2a gaps it stood on. With this, every R2 prompt
has landed; what is left of R2 is its exit playtest.

## What was built

- **`scripts/world/Raven.gd`** — per villain, with `villain`, `world_sites`, `fog` and
  `marker_parent` as fields. At each dawn: if fewer than **3** pings are outstanding, a **70%** roll;
  on success it names one site from `WorldSites.undiscovered_eligible()`, at random. If nothing
  qualifies it emits `raven_silent`, and the 2026-08-29 ruling gives that a face: the HUD chip
  flickers "Raven —" and the log says *"The bird found nothing. The near country is picked
  clean."* A failed 70% roll is simply a day without news. Pings never expire; looting the site,
  or anything else that spends its last charge, claims the ping.
- **`scripts/world/RavenMarker.gd`** — a violet sigil over the site at z 110, above the fog at 100.
  It reveals nothing. It is inspectable as *"The Raven's Word"*: site, band, the day found,
  distance as the raven flies, and the line that she vouches for the destination, never the road.
- **HUD chip** (`HudTopBar.raven_chip`, under the portrait): hidden until there is news, reads
  "Raven ×N", and pulses on a new ping. A click centres the camera on the newest unseen ping and
  then cycles through the rest. It counts as a manual pan, so villain-follow drops
  (`GameCamera.center_on_manual`), and it opens the mark's panel.
- **Minimap**: violet diamonds, drawn above the fog. The header comment records why this is the one
  shipped exception to "no live contents".
- **EventBus**: `raven_pinged`, `raven_ping_seen`, `raven_ping_claimed`, `raven_silent`.

## The honesty invariant, as built

`WorldSites.raven_checks(site)` returns the five conditions separately: reachable, band ≤ 2,
unguarded and unoccupied, undiscovered, and at least one charge left. A site must also carry
`raven_eligible: true` in `world_sites.json`. The flag says *may*; the checks say *must*. Today the
flag is on both fresh graves, the hidden cache and the abandoned camp.

**Reachability moved into `WorldSites.is_reachable()`** as a single flood fill from the lair,
computed once. The F3 overlay now asks it too, so there is one answer to "can he get there".

## "Undiscovered" — a deliberate reading of the spec

§8 says the fog answers "has the player found this", but the lair band is revealed from the first
frame. Read that way, the two fresh graves and the hidden cache would never be pingable, and the
Raven would have at most one site (the camp) per run. So discovery is its own flag:
`WorldSite.discovered`, set by `WorldSites.update_discovery()` when the Necromancer or one of his units
stands within **the same sight disc that lights the fog** (7 cells for him, 3 for a unit, plus the
Barrow Lantern). Main checks it every 0.25 s. It is read-only over the fog: the harness asserts the
fog is byte-identical across 1,000 pings. At the start of a run he has found only the hollow grave,
which is the one the opening line points at. The Raven's first dawns name the scree grave, the
hidden cache and, if it rolled empty, the camp.

## The R2a gaps this closed

- **Camp occupancy** (LOOT_SITES_SPEC §2/§3): `abandoned_camp` carries
  `occupancy: {chance: 0.35, kind: "outlaw", count: 1}`. It is rolled once at activation. An occupied
  camp posts an ordinary outlaw guardian (hidden by the fog until approached), and clearing it is
  the usual `cleared_a_site` deed. The Raven honestly knows an empty camp when the player can't.
- **Ruling 1 applied to the camp**: gold 44 / essence 18 / wood 8 (1) / food 8 (1–2) / arms 8 /
  uncommon relic 4, over 4 rolls. `verify_loot_tables` exempts the camp's mundane column and asserts
  gold-dominance instead, as it does for the cache.
- **Wolf den gold weight 26 → 30.** Its mean gold sat exactly on the Band 2 floor (≈2.0), so
  `verify_loot_tables` passed or failed on sampling noise. The new camp table shifted the seeded
  sequence and it failed. The den now averages ≈2.25 gold. Found, not wanted: flag it if the den
  should stay leaner.

## Harness

`tools/verify_raven.tscn`, 39 assertions:
- The five invariant conditions as separate counts over 1,000 dawns. For those dawns every
  lootable site, Band 3–4 included, is made a candidate by flag, and discovery, guards and charges
  are scrambled each dawn, so the filter does the work.
- Occupancy rolls near 35% over 2,000 activations; an occupied camp is never pinged and an empty one
  is eligible.
- The cap holds, silence fires `raven_silent` with nothing invented, and cadence lands near 70%.
- The fog is byte-identical after 1,000 pings; claiming clears the mark from world and minimap; the
  mark is above the fog; the chip drops follow and opens the panel.

Two older assertions changed. `check_fog_and_minimap` now checks that F3 moves no site's
`discovered` flag, instead of checking that no such flag exists. `verify_loot_tables`'s cache
channel test accepts "spent, or holding only what his hands could not take", because two rolls of
2–4 gold can exceed his 6. It had only passed on seeds that rolled low.

**Observed** in a windowed run under Xvfb at 60×: dawns 2–4 pinged the scree grave and the hidden
cache; the chip read "Raven ×2", and a click centred on the cache and opened the panel.
